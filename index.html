Your phone number is tied to your name. Every app that asks for it can trace you.

Since the SIM Registration Act, every mobile number in the Philippines is linked to a government ID. Any messenger that signs you up with that number can connect your chats to who you are. If that data leaks, it leaks with your name attached.

Frequently asked questions

Is Privy free to use? +

Yes. Downloading Privy and creating your ID costs nothing, and there is no registration fee.

How do you register without a phone number? +

When you open the app, Privy generates a random ID on your device. You share that ID with people you want to talk to, instead of a phone number. We never ask for your number, email, or real name.

Is it really end-to-end encrypted by default? +

Yes. All chats use the Signal Protocol from the first message, and there is no setting to enable. Messages are locked on your device and can only be opened by the person you sent them to.

How does Verified Business work? +

Sellers can apply for a Verified Business badge. Once approved, the badge appears on their profile so buyers can tell them apart from anonymous accounts. Buyers don't need to verify anything to use Privy.
