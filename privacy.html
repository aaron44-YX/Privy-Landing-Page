Last updated 09/30/2026

This is a plain-language notice, not a legal document. If you want it reviewed by someone qualified before you rely on it, that's a good idea.

Who we are

Privy is an independent project run by Aaron Porlas in the Philippines. Contact: aaronporlas140@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your email address; whether you ticked the box to help test on Android; the tag of the link you arrived from (for example "fb-group"); and the date and time you joined.

This page also uses a privacy-friendly page-view counter to see roughly how many people visit and which links they came from. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't collect personal data, and isn't Google Analytics or a Meta Pixel.

Why

To email you about Privy's launch and to invite testers. Our basis for this is your consent.

Who sees it

Only me. When I send emails, the email service I use (Gmail) processes your address on my behalf. I do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Until Privy's first public release plus 90 days, or until you ask me to delete it, whichever comes first.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 you may ask to access, correct, or delete your data, withdraw your consent at any time, and complain to the National Privacy Commission. Email aaronporlas140@gmail.com and I will respond within 30 days.

Security

The list is stored in a database that only I can read. Exports are kept encrypted.
